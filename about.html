Hi, I'm Sufian Rasheed — a digital marketer from Lahore, Pakistan.

I started in marketing in 2022 and have spent the last 4+ years helping brands get seen, get chosen and keep growing online. My clients range from local businesses in Pakistan to brands overseas.

My work covers the full journey: planning content people actually want to watch, running Meta and Google ad campaigns, improving websites for search, and setting up the tracking that shows where every lead came from.

Before marketing, I spent years in office operations and data work. That habit stayed with me — I like neat reports, clear numbers and decisions backed by evidence.
