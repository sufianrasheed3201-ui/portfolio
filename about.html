About me

My story

Hi, I'm Sufian Rasheed, a digital marketer from Lahore, Pakistan.

I started in marketing in 2022. Since then I have helped brands get seen, get chosen and keep growing online, from local businesses in Pakistan to clients overseas.

My work covers the whole journey: planning content people actually want to watch, running Meta and Google ad campaigns, improving websites for search, and setting up the tracking that shows where every lead came from.

Before marketing I spent years in office operations and data work. That habit stayed with me: I like neat reports, clear numbers and decisions backed by evidence.

I also look after marketing for M Tech Textile Traders, our family business, and run Navifex Pro, my small digital marketing studio.
